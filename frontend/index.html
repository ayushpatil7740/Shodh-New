How Lost & Found Works

A simple, secure process to safely reunite belongings with students and staff.

1️⃣

1. Report Details

Fill out the form with item name, category, location, date, and upload a clear photo.

2️⃣

2. Smart Matching

Our algorithm compares category, title, location, and dates to compute potential matches.

3️⃣

3. Safe Recovery

Reach out through verified contact details and safely retrieve your item on campus.
